Privacy policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Wyticle AI may process when you use the website or app, connect Google services, generate content, or publish an article. The exact information processed depends on the features you choose to use.

1. Information we process

  • Account and sign-in information: when you sign in, the identity provider may provide your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier.
  • Projects and drafts: project history and drafts are stored in your browser or device storage (including IndexedDB) where the app uses local storage. Removing browser/app data may remove locally stored projects.
  • Content you submit: prompts, article briefs, research instructions, and text or settings you choose to send for generation or publishing.
  • Connected services: if you authorize Blogger or Google Drive, the app processes the permissions and data needed to provide the feature you requested. OAuth tokens are handled by the backend and are intended to be stored securely.
  • Operational and billing data: technical request information, usage/credit records, and payment status may be processed to operate the service, prevent abuse, and reconcile purchases.

2. How we use information

Information is used to authenticate you, provide writing and research features, save your settings and projects, connect and publish to services you authorize, process usage or payments, secure the service, diagnose errors, and respond to support requests.

3. AI and third-party providers

When you request AI generation, the relevant prompt and content are sent to the configured AI provider through the service backend. Google services may be used for sign-in, Blogger publishing, or Drive features you authorize. Hosting, database, authentication, and payment providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services. Those providers' own privacy terms also apply.

4. Google permissions and revocation

Google access is requested for the features you choose, such as sign-in, Blogger, or Drive. You can review or revoke third-party access from your Google Account security settings. Revoking access may stop connected features from working; it does not necessarily delete information already stored in your device or service records.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Some drafts may remain only on your device; connected-service tokens, account records, usage data, and operational records may be stored by the backend and its configured database providers. Retention depends on the purpose, technical requirements, and applicable law. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers, shared with a service you explicitly connect or publish to, or disclosed where reasonably necessary to protect the service, comply with law, or address security and fraud concerns.

7. Your choices

You can choose whether to sign in, which integrations to authorize, what content to submit, and where to publish. You can clear local browser/app storage to remove locally stored data, disconnect integrations where supported, and revoke Google permissions. For a request about backend-held data, use the app's Help & feedback feature and identify the account and data involved. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

8. Children and changes

Wyticle AI is not intended for children under 13, and users must meet any higher minimum age required in their jurisdiction. We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Help & feedback feature within Wyticle AI. Do not include passwords, OAuth client secrets, or other credentials in a support request.
